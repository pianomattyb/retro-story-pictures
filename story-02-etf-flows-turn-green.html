ETF Flows Turn Green

US spot bitcoin ETFs took in a net $7.9 million on Monday, ending a seven-session outflow run totalling about $1.91 billion. BlackRock's IBIT drew $76.5 million to snap an 11-session losing streak, though short-term holders are still selling at a loss and rate fears linger.
