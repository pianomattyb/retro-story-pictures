Huang Under Fire

Nvidia shares fell more than 4% on Monday as the board's independent investigation into CEO Jensen Huang continued. President Donald Trump said he spoke with Huang on Monday, with Huang denying any knowledge that Alice Huang was a spy; a shareholder class action has been filed, and three House committee chairs have requested a closed-door briefing on November 18. The board says it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.
