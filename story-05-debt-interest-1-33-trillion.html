Debt Interest Hits $1.33 Trillion

Annual interest payments on US public debt hit a record $1.33T and will soon pass Social Security as the largest line item in the federal budget. Over the last 10 years, US tax revenue rose 64% to $5.4T while government spending rose 98% to $7.5T, more than doubling the national debt to $40.5T.
