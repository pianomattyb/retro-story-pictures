Hot CPI Revives Hike Talk

US consumer prices rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% forecast, with core CPI up 0.3% for a third straight month. Traders fully priced a quarter-point December hike, and odds of a half-point move jumped to about 13.6% after Fed Governor Waller said a 50bp hike should be considered.
