Banks Double Down

13F filings show Goldman Sachs held about $1.283 billion in spot bitcoin ETFs on September 30, up from $580 million in June, and Wells Fargo reported $2.35 billion in BlackRock's IBIT, up from $812 million. The filings are a snapshot from before the October-November selloff.
