Bitcoin Bounces Back

Bitcoin fell to about $73,700 late on November 9, its lowest since August, in a slide that liquidated more than $1 billion in leveraged positions. It rebounded above $75,000 and was trading near $74,500 on the morning of November 10, holding above its 200-day moving average near $73,150, even as spot ETFs recorded $411.9 million in outflows on November 6 and $1.28 billion for the week.
