Nvidia Boots Its Founder

Nvidia's board is reportedly preparing to oust founder and CEO Jensen Huang, and shares plunged 8 percent in after-hours trading on the news. Huang has led Nvidia since co-founding it in 1993 and built it into the world's most valuable chipmaker on the AI boom. His sudden departure would shake the company at the peak of the AI hardware cycle.
