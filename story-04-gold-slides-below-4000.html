Gold Slides Below $4,000

Gold has fallen below $4,000 to a more than one-week low as the dollar climbs to a 19-month high. Traders are pricing in growing odds of a Federal Reserve rate hike in December after hotter-than-expected inflation data. A stronger dollar and higher rate expectations are a double headwind for bullion, which pays no yield.
