The Twenty-Year Lock

The House Financial Services Committee formally reported H.R. 8957, the American Reserve Modernization Act, sending it to the full House; the bill would write the Strategic Bitcoin Reserve into law and bar the Treasury from selling its bitcoin for at least 20 years.
