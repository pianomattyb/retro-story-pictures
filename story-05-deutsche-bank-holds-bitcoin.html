Deutsche Bank Holds Bitcoin

Deutsche Bank has launched Bitcoin and Ether custody services for its institutional clients, bringing crypto safekeeping inside one of Europe's largest banking empires. The move gives pension funds, asset managers and other big investors a regulated, household-name custodian for digital assets. It is the latest sign that traditional banking is absorbing crypto rather than fighting it.
